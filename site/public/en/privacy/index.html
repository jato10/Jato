Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information Global Beyond LLC (“the company”) collects through globalbeyondllc.com and how it uses it.

Information we collect

The company collects the information the user voluntarily submits through the site's forms: name, email address, phone number and the content of the message or review.

The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses aggregate visit and performance measurements provided by Vercel, which work without cookies and without identifying the user. The browser stores locally a technical flag for the chosen language; it contains no personal data and is not sent to the company.

Use of information

The company uses the information to read and answer the message or review received and to keep a record of the communication. It does not use it for advertising. It does not sell or rent it.

Third parties

Messages are delivered by email through Resend, and the site is hosted on Vercel. These providers process the information to provide their service, under their own terms and policies.

Links to WhatsApp and Instagram, Meta services, are governed by the policies of those services. The company does not control how they handle data.

The company may disclose information when required by law or by a competent authority.

Retention

The company keeps the information for as long as needed to answer the communication, keep records and meet legal obligations.

User rights

The user may request access to, correction of or deletion of their information by writing through the contact form or on WhatsApp. The company will handle the request in accordance with applicable law.

Security

The company applies reasonable measures to protect the information. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

Minors

The site is not directed to children under 13 and the company does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

The company may update this policy. The update date shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy are sent through the contact form or on WhatsApp.
