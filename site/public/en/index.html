What we do

Three ways to work with us.

Retail, wholesale, and catalog requests — each one answered by a person, not a queue.

Retail

Individual orders, handled personally. Tell us what you are looking for and we confirm what is currently available, what it costs, and how it ships.

Wholesale

Volume requests reviewed case by case. Share quantities, specifications, and timelines, and we come back with availability and pricing for that request.

Catalog requests

Our selection changes over time. Ask for the current catalog and you receive what is genuinely available now — not an outdated page.

How it works

A short, transparent path from question to order.

Four steps. The same people with you through all of them.

  1. 01

    Send your request

    Write to us with what you need — a product, a category, a quantity, or simply the current catalog.

  2. 02

    We review availability

  3. 03

  4. 04

    We stay with your order

The selection

A catalog that evolves.

We do not publish a fixed product grid, because that is not how our selection works. What we offer is curated and it changes — new items arrive, others move on.

So instead of showing you a page that might already be out of date, we send you the current catalog when you ask for it, with the availability and pricing that apply on that day.

Wholesale & bulk orders

Larger orders, reviewed properly.

Bulk and wholesale requests are quoted individually. To give you an accurate answer on the first reply, it helps to include:

01

Quantities

How many units, and whether the volume is one-time or recurring.

02

Specifications

Products, categories, variants, or references you already have in mind.

03

Timeline

When you need it, and how flexible those dates are.

04

Destination

Where the order is going, so we can confirm shipping details for it.
